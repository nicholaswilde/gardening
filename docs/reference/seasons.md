# Seasonal Definitions

**Status:** Reference Document
**Purpose:** Standardizing month ranges for the `Season Planted` attribute in crop profiles.

## :calendar: Planting Seasons

| Planting Term | Month Range | Cultivation Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Early Spring** | February – March | Soil is warming. Ideal for starting warm-season seeds (peppers, tomatoes) indoors and planting hardy starts. |
| **Mid-Spring** | April | Primary transplanting window. Danger of unexpected cold snaps has passed. |
| **Late Spring** | May | Rapid growth phase. Last viable window to transplant before high summer heat sets in. |
| **Early Summer** | June | Transition to heat-tolerant crops and heavy mulch applications to retain moisture. |
| **Mid-Summer** | July – August | Peak heat. Focus shifts from planting to irrigation management and harvesting. |
| **Late Summer** | September | Preparing beds for the cool-season transition. Sowing fall crops in shade. |
| **Early Fall** | October | Second major planting window. Ideal for establishing winter cover crops (wheat) and cool-weather herbs. |
| **Mid-Fall** | November | Slower growth. Excellent time for planting perennial herbs (rosemary, chives) to establish deep roots. |
| **Late Fall / Winter** | December – January | Deepest cool season. Focus on bed maintenance, hardware repair, and indoor starting. |

## :pushpin: Notes

- When a crop spans multiple ranges (e.g., planted in late February), default to the earlier term (Early Spring) for consistency in the metadata.
